Homora privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Homora checks the home network your phone is connected to: which devices are on it, whether it has common security problems, and how strong your Wi-Fi signal is. This policy explains what stays on your phone and what, if anything, is sent to us.

The short version

  • Your device list stays on your phone. Device names, IP and MAC addresses, Wi-Fi network names, signal readings and room names are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Clear saved data.
  • We never collect your location. Android requires location permission for apps to read the Wi-Fi name and see nearby networks. Homora uses that permission only for those Wi-Fi details and never reads, stores or sends your location.
  • Usage statistics are off unless you turn them on, and never include anything about your network or devices.

What Homora does on your network

When you tap Scan, Homora looks only at the private network your phone is on (at most 254 addresses). It asks devices which services they offer and what they are called, the same way your phone finds a printer or a TV. It never tries passwords, never sends exploits, never disconnects devices, and never scans other networks. Scans run only while the app is open. If you turn on New device alerts, a short check runs at most once a day, only on Wi-Fi while your phone is charging.

These are processed with Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Firestore, Authentication and App Check). Firebase also records an app-instance identifier, your phone model and Android version, and an approximate country derived from your IP address. Advertising ID collection and ad personalisation are switched off. Feedback is linked to an anonymous sign-in identifier, not to your name or Google account. See how Firebase handles data.

Homora never sends device names, IP or MAC addresses, Wi-Fi network names, signal readings, room names or your location. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Turn usage sharing on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Delete everything stored on your phone with Settings → Clear saved data, or by uninstalling.
  • To have feedback you sent deleted, e-mail us at the address below.

Retention

Analytics data is kept for the shortest period Firebase allows. Feedback is kept until the issue is handled and then deleted, or sooner on request.

Children

Homora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
